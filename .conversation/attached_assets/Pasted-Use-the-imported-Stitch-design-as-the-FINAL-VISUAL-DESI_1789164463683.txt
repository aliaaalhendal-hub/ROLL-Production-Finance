PRODUCT
==================================================

ROLL is a FinTech platform for film and commercial production.

It manages:

- production budgets
- expenses
- committed costs
- production contracts
- payment schedules
- payment requests
- production payments
- cash flow
- financial forecasting
- AI-powered production decision analysis

The core idea:

ROLL helps production companies understand the financial impact of a production decision BEFORE approving it.
